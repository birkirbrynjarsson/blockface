Block hour advances every 6 blocks, block tick every block (~10m apart) — both only move when a real block arrives. The counter next to it is a stopwatch since that block, uncapped, so a late block just keeps counting past 10:00. Offset shows how far that's drifted from where wall-clock midnight would put it.
