Block hour and block tick advance every 6 / every block (~10m apart) based on confirmed blocks; the small counter is the minute and second since that block. Past 10 minutes with no new block, the tick turns red with a small "+N" for the ten-minute chunks carried over — read together with the small counter, e.g. "+2" and "5:43" is 25:43 since the block. Offset shows how far that's drifted from where wall-clock midnight would put it.
